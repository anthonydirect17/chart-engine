The light

A slow comet around a panel's border, with a soft glow. On the trading screen and The Desk it runs only while you are in a trade, only around the panel showing that trade: green in profit, red under water, none when flat. On the Agent tab it follows what the agent is doing. Only the light's colour fades between states; numbers never move.
